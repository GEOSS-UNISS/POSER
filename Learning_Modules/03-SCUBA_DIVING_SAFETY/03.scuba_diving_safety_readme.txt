This is a  bridge between Blender camera animation and Subsurface dive profiles. 
Cinematic motion paths are turned into simulated scuba diving log.

A timing model based on underwater speed (speed_ms) makes the generated dive physically plausible.
With realistic ascent phases:
bottom descent
ascent to safety stop
3-minute stop at 6 m
final ascent

These values are customizable in the script called "Export_CSV_to_Subsurface.py" available in Poser/Scripts/Subsurface
