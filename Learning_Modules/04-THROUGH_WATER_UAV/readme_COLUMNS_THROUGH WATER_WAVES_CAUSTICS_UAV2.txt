This scene simulates an UAV image acquisition over a submerged shipwreck cargo considering the effect of refraction at the water surface.
The refraction produces geometric distortions and caustics at the seabed.
Usage:

1) Select and adjust the parameters of the ocean modifier to increase or decrease the effect of the waves
2) Adjust rendering resolution and camera parameters as needed 
3) Render the image sequence using Ctrl + F12 or from the Render menu 
4) Images are rendered in a subfolder
