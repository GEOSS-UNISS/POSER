This scene enables the volume scattering rendering to simulate light attenuation and backscattering.
Practictionners can experiment with varying the different attenuation coefficient and backscattering intensity.

1) On the left 3D viewport set the viewport shading to "Material Preview" or "Rendered"
2) Modify the attenuation/backscatter parameters in the shading viewport
3) Interact with lights, ports and other objects in the outliner

Excercises:

A) optimize the left and right light orientations to minimize backscatter (hint--> select each light in the outliner and change its rotation in the item transform panel). 
B) vary the distance to the object to reduce the attenuation (hint --> move/ resize the path).
